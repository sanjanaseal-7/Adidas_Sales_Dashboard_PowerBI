# Adidas_Sales_Dashboard_PowerBI

# 1. Project Overview:

I created this dashboard to analyse Adidas sales performance and identify key trends across sales, profitability, products, regions, retailers, and time periods. The dashboard provides an interactive view of business performance to support data-driven sales and strategic decision-making.

# 3. Tools & Skills:

Excel | Power Query | Data Modelling | Data Visualization | Power BI | Dashboard Design

# 2. Key Insights Covered:

• Total Sales: $900M

• Operating Profit: $332M

• Units Sold: 2M

• Average Price per Unit: $45.22

• Average Operating Margin: 42%

• Analysed monthly sales trends and performance.

• Compared sales performance across U.S. states and regions.

• Evaluated sales contribution by product category.

• Analysed sales performance across major retail partners.

# 4. Business Insights:

• Identified monthly sales trends to understand periods of stronger and weaker performance.

• Analysed regional and state-level sales to identify high-performing markets and potential growth opportunities.

• Compared product categories to understand which products contribute most to overall revenue.

• Evaluated retailer performance to identify key sales channels and their contribution to revenue.

• Used a data-driven analysis and problem-solving approach to identify performance gaps, trends, and potential areas for business growth.
